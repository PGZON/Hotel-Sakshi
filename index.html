Our Story

Tradition Meets Innovation

Founded in 2005, Hotel Sakshi has been serving authentic flavors and culinary excellence to our valued customers for over 15 years. Our journey began with a simple passion for food and hospitality.

At Hotel Sakshi, we believe in the perfect blend of traditional recipes and modern culinary techniques. Each dish is crafted with care, using locally sourced ingredients and age-old family recipes passed down through generations.

Our team of experienced chefs and friendly staff are committed to providing you with an unforgettable dining experience in a warm and welcoming atmosphere.
